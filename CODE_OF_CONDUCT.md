# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and is better
informed than you about their own situation.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the code and the argument, not on the person making it.
- Accept that reasonable people disagree about design, and that the maintainer gets the
  final call on scope.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, fix it, and move on without ceremony.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments — public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeatedly reopening settled decisions.

## Enforcement

Report anything that concerns you to **karanmjpinto@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
privately and the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments and code that violate this document,
and may block accounts for serious or repeated violations. Enforcement decisions are
explained where doing so does not compromise someone's privacy.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit
comments — and when representing the project in public.
